Executive Finance Project 

I started with raw financial data — **Revenue, Expenses, Profit, and Dates** — spread across spreadsheets. Using **Power Query**, I cleaned and shaped the data, turning messy tables into a structured model. Then, I connected the dots: linking products to revenue, expenses to categories, and building a solid foundation for analysis.

With the model ready, I created **DAX measures** that tell the real story:
- Total Revenue, Expenses, Profit, and Profit Margin  
- Growth trends (Month‑over‑Month, Year‑over‑Year)  
- Product performance and expense breakdowns  

Finally, I designed an **executive‑ready dashboard**:
- KPI cards at the top for quick decision‑making  
- Trend charts to show revenue and profit over time  
- Product revenue comparisons (Laptop, Phone, Tablet, Accessories)  
- Expense breakdowns by Salary, Marketing, IT, and Operations  
- Clean formatting, icons, and slicers for interactivity  

---

## 🎯 Business Value
This dashboard transforms raw numbers into **actionable insights**.  
Executives can instantly see:
- Whether profits are rising or falling  
- Which products drive the most revenue  
- Which categories consume the most expenses  
- How growth compares across months and years  

It’s not just a dashboard — it’s a **story of the company’s financial health**.
